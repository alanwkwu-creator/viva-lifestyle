The Circle

Membership

Join the Circle.

Membership is what keeps the network alive between events. Start free — genuinely free, no card, no trial that quietly bills you. Move up whenever you're ready, or don't. Plenty of people stay on Open for years.

Open

Free forever

For anyone aged 15 to 30 living in Hong Kong. This is where 80% of members are, and it's a complete membership, not a teaser.

What Open includes is public. What it leads to is not.

Active

HK$480 / year

About HK$40 a month — one lunch. For people who are already coming to two or three things a month and want first pick.

Priority, kit, and two seats at a table you have not seen.

Builder

By application

For members actually building something — a company, a team, a project with users. Free if you're pre-revenue. We'd rather have you in than have your money.

If you are building something, we already want to talk.
