The Circle

Membership

The Circle.

Membership is by invitation. Members are introduced by an existing member, or found by us. Enquiries are welcome, and are answered by a person.

Associate

By invitation

For those recently introduced. The greater part of the network is here.

What Associate includes is stated. What it leads to is not.

Member

Annual dues HK$480

For those who are here often. Dues cover kit, priority, and a seat at two tables a year.

Priority, and two seats at a table you have not seen.

Fellow

By nomination

By nomination, for members building something of consequence. There is no fee.

If you are building something, we are likely already aware.
