The Circle · Enquiries

Membership is by invitation.

Most members are introduced. If nobody has introduced you, write to us instead.

There is no application form and no scoring. An enquiry is a letter to a person, and it is answered by one.

Membership is not sold. Dues exist so that the Member tier covers its own costs, and bursaries exist so that they never decide who belongs.

We are a youth organisation. Everyone here is between fifteen and thirty, and members under eighteen need a guardian's consent before their first event.

Partners, venues and incubators should write to the same address. We answer those first.

What follows an invitation is described only to those who receive one.
